# Mactician 1.3.6 — TFT is back on Mac

Riot restored native TFT support for Macs with M-series chips in patch 18.7.
You can now download the official client directly from Riot. [Download TFT for Mac](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/download/) · [Read Riot’s patch notes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-7/)

Thank you for playing with Mactician. I’m ending regular support for the launcher. If you still rely on it or something breaks, email me. I’m open to picking it up again. [Email Sergei](mailto:tweet9ra@gmail.com)

- Choose **Play with Mactician** on the welcome screen to open your existing launcher and game settings.
- If you’d like to thank me for building and updating Mactician, you can leave a tip. [Support the developer](https://sergeinaumov.dev/mactician/donate)
