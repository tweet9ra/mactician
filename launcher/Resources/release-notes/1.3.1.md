# Mactician 1.3.1 — TFT 18.4 performance update

- Enable the Vulkan buffer-view cache for Global TFT 18.4-5637330, reducing
  repeated graphics-driver work while preserving your graphics settings.
- Bundle the verified Global TFT 18.4 inputs for new installations.
- Add Taiwan alongside Global and Vietnam (VNG), with independent downloads,
  game updates, and sign-in.

Eight local measurements in one Tocker’s Trials planning scene on M1 Max at
2560×1440 improved median FPS from 44.44 to 60.00 (+35.0%) and reduced median
p95 frame time from 34.09 to 18.50 ms. Results vary by Mac and scene; this is
not a late-match or PvP performance guarantee. The cache remains restricted
to verified Global builds and is not enabled for Vietnam or Taiwan.

Your existing runtime, game data, and settings are preserved when updating.
